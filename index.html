Why speak out loud

Reading answers silently is not rehearsal.

Under pressure, pace accelerates and filler phrases creep in between thoughts. Hearing and measuring your spoken rhythm makes weak spots obvious in one take.

Common questions

Is my voice recording stored? +

No. Speech is transcribed in your browser, and your session analysis stays in your browser's local storage so your report page can load it.

Which browsers work for live voice? +

Chrome and Edge support live Web Speech transcription and microphone audio reactivity. In Firefox or Safari you can type or paste your answer and run the exact same pacing analysis.

How is the score calculated? +

It weighs speaking pace (ideal range 110–170 words per minute), filler-word density across 15 common phrases, and whether your answer has enough substance to include an example.

Is the studio free to use? +

Yes — the practice studio and instant delivery report are free to try with no account needed.
