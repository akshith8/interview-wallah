Questions

Is my voice recording stored? +

In this demo, speech is transcribed in your browser and nothing is saved.

Which browsers work for voice? +

Chrome and Edge support live speech recognition. In other browsers you can paste or type your answer instead.

How is the score calculated? +

It combines your speaking pace (ideal 120 to 170 words per minute), how many filler words you use, and answer length.
